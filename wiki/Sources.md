# Sources

Rung keeps sources in two groups because they support different kinds of claims:

1. **Learning-science research** — evidence about learning mechanisms.
2. **MAPS_Lean primary documents** — design sources from which Rung adapts process-control ideas.

A MAPS document is a source for **where a Rung design idea came from**; it is not evidence that the educational adaptation has been empirically validated.

---

# Learning-science research

## Retrieval practice / testing effect

**Roediger, H. L. III, & Karpicke, J. D. (2006).** Test-Enhanced Learning: Taking Memory Tests Improves Long-Term Retention. *Psychological Science, 17*(3), 249–255.  
DOI: [10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x)

**Used in Rung for:** delayed retrieval checks and the rule that rereading/familiarity should not be confused with recall.

---

## Self-explanation

**Chi, M. T. H., Bassok, M., Lewis, M. W., Reimann, P., & Glaser, R. (1989).** Self-Explanations: How Students Study and Use Examples in Learning to Solve Problems. *Cognitive Science, 13*(2), 145–182.  
DOI: [10.1207/s15516709cog1302_1](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1302_1)

**Used in Rung for:** asking learners to explain why steps and principles connect, rather than accepting answer production alone.

**Chi, M. T. H., de Leeuw, N., Chiu, M.-H., & LaVancher, C. (1994).** Eliciting Self-Explanations Improves Understanding. *Cognitive Science, 18*(3), 439–477.  
DOI: [10.1207/s15516709cog1803_3](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1803_3)

**Used in Rung for:** deliberately prompting self-explanation as part of the teaching loop.

---

## Cognitive load and instructional guidance

**Paas, F., & van Merriënboer, J. J. G. (2020).** Cognitive-Load Theory: Methods to Manage Working Memory Load in the Learning of Complex Tasks. *Current Directions in Psychological Science, 29*(4), 394–398.  
DOI: [10.1177/0963721420922183](https://doi.org/10.1177/0963721420922183)

**Used in Rung for:** the principle that assistance should account for limited working memory and task complexity.

**Kirschner, P. A., Sweller, J., & Clark, R. E. (2006).** Why Minimal Guidance During Instruction Does Not Work: An Analysis of the Failure of Constructivist, Discovery, Problem-Based, Experiential, and Inquiry-Based Teaching. *Educational Psychologist, 41*(2), 75–86.  
DOI: [10.1207/s15326985ep4102_1](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15326985ep4102_1)

**Used in Rung for:** rejecting an absolute "questions only / never explain" interpretation of Socratic teaching, especially for novices lacking prerequisite knowledge.

---

## Expertise reversal / guidance fading

**Kalyuga, S., Ayres, P., Chandler, P., & Sweller, J. (2003).** The Expertise Reversal Effect. *Educational Psychologist, 38*(1), 23–31.  
DOI: [10.1207/S15326985EP3801_4](https://doi.org/10.1207/S15326985EP3801_4)

**Used in Rung for:** fading support as competence increases rather than using one fixed instructional intensity for every learner.

---

## Productive failure

**Kapur, M. (2014).** Productive Failure in Learning Math. *Cognitive Science, 38*(5), 1008–1022.  
DOI: [10.1111/cogs.12107](https://doi.org/10.1111/cogs.12107)

**Used in Rung for:** allowing a meaningful attempt before full explanation when the learner has enough prerequisite knowledge for the attempt to generate useful reasoning.

**Important limit:** Rung does not infer from this that all failure or unguided struggle is beneficial.

# MAPS_Lean primary documents

Repository: [BigCatMellow/MAPS_Lean](https://github.com/BigCatMellow/MAPS_Lean)

## Project Bootstrap

[`playbook/PROJECT_BOOTSTRAP.md`](https://github.com/BigCatMellow/MAPS_Lean/blob/main/playbook/PROJECT_BOOTSTRAP.md)

Key ideas adapted:

- inspect reality before planning;
- define observable DONE;
- separate verified facts from assumptions;
- use checkpoints;
- change the plan when evidence changes.

Rung translations:

- baseline before teaching;
- observable independent mastery;
- periodic learning-steering checks.

---

## Agent-Grade Instructions Standard

[`playbook/AGI_STANDARD.md`](https://github.com/BigCatMellow/MAPS_Lean/blob/main/playbook/AGI_STANDARD.md)

Key ideas adapted:

- `VERIFIED / REPORTED / ASSUMED / UNKNOWN`;
- observable acceptance criteria;
- explicit verification/evidence;
- stop/escalation conditions;
- clarity should remove consequential guessing.

Rung translations:

- distinguish demonstrated competence from reported confidence;
- define mastery evidence;
- know when questioning should give way to explanation or research.

## Task Lifecycle

[`playbook/TASK_LIFECYCLE.md`](https://github.com/BigCatMellow/MAPS_Lean/blob/main/playbook/TASK_LIFECYCLE.md)

Key ideas adapted:

- shape before executing;
- completion requires acceptance criteria and verification;
- re-shape when material assumptions change.

Rung translations:

- define the learning target before teaching;
- do not call guided success mastery;
- change the teaching plan when a prerequisite or misconception changes the situation.

---

## Repair and Learning

[`playbook/REPAIR_AND_LEARNING.md`](https://github.com/BigCatMellow/MAPS_Lean/blob/main/playbook/REPAIR_AND_LEARNING.md)

Key ideas adapted:

- diagnose before repair;
- distinguish one-off repair from structural/repeated failure;
- repeated failure deserves a durable countermeasure;
- test the countermeasure against the motivating failure.

Rung translations:

- classify learner errors;
- convert recurring mistakes into diagnostic questions, principles, checklists, or drills.

## Information Lifecycle

[`playbook/INFORMATION_LIFECYCLE.md`](https://github.com/BigCatMellow/MAPS_Lean/blob/main/playbook/INFORMATION_LIFECYCLE.md)

Key ideas adapted:

- keep current truth easy to find;

- explicitly retire or supersede stale information;

Rung translation:

## Agent Operating Contract

[`AGENTS.md`](https://github.com/BigCatMellow/MAPS_Lean/blob/main/AGENTS.md)

Key ideas adapted:

- use the smallest sufficient method;
- do not silently expand scope;
- do not hide uncertainty;
- do not manufacture extra work after success.

Rung translations:

- minimum necessary assistance;
- tangent control;
- use `UNKNOWN` when facts are unresolved;
- stop a learning arc when its defined mastery proof passes.
